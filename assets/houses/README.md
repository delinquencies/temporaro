# Watch-house explorer imagery

The directory has 69 individual profiles and official-site links. Eight houses remain initially featured; search and the A–Z control expose all 69. There are 43 illustrated profiles and 26 deliberate typographic treatments where a suitable photograph was not obtained.

This pass added 35 photographs: 32 from manufacturer websites and three from the GPHG archive (F.P. Journe, MB&F and Kari Voutilainen). No Wikipedia or Wikimedia requests were made in this pass. Existing images include official-site photographs, a client-supplied Patek Philippe reference and Tudor's unmodified EMore98 Commons photograph under CC BY-SA 4.0. The Tudor source and license remain visible.

`sources.json` records each displayed file, source page and credit. Image-source links appear in the explorer. The added photos are local design-preview assets; publication permissions have not been established. Credits are not licenses. Profiles do not assert stock or manufacturer affiliation.

New photographs are local WebP derivatives, at most 1200 pixels on either axis. The Longines derivative is 116,052 bytes, replacing a 7,563,682-byte PNG in both explorer and gallery. Research originals and rejected candidates are archived outside the public site in `../../../research-originals/`.

The inquiry is still a copy-only preview. Starting an inquiry passes the selected house and optional collection without overwriting client contact details or notes.
